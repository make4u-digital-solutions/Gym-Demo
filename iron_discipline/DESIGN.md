## Brand & Style
This design system embodies a premium, elite bodybuilding and high-performance training culture. The mood is unapologetic, commanding, and cinematic—channeling the intense atmosphere of an underground iron sanctum with luxury execution. Visuals prioritize high muscular definition, deep chiaroscuro lighting, and fiery combustion tones over sterile wellness tropes.

The aesthetic fuses aggressive brutalism with precision high-contrast dark mode. Sharp structural alignments, angled chamfers, tactical geometric framing, and focused atmospheric illumination create a visceral sense of strength and relentless momentum. It evokes discipline, grit, and belonging to an uncompromising cadre of athletes.

## Colors
The palette is built on deep obsidian tones pierced by incandescent kinetic warmth:

- **Deep Black (`#080808`)**: The foundational canvas; establishes maximum cinematic contrast and deep shadow depth.
- **Dark Charcoal (`#151515`)**: Structural surface tier for containers, card backings, and tactical panels.
- **Border Muted (`#262626`)**: Subtle, rigid architectural perimeter borders.
- **Primary Scarlet (`#F04435`)**: High-impact energetic accent used for primary CTAs, critical alerts, and brand hero moments.
- **Intense Ember (`#FF941A`)**: Kinetic gradient partner to Primary Scarlet; highlights active states, badges, and dual-tone typographic lockups.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp, authoritative punch for primary headlines, high-priority icons, and maximum emphasis.
- **Secondary Gray (`#A6A6A6`)**: Controlled neutral for supporting body copy, metadata, and structural guides to prevent visual fatigue.

## Typography
Display typography is tall, condensed, and architectural. All headline levels are set in uppercase to maximize visual density and discipline. Letterforms stack cleanly with negligible line spacing to evoke raw solid iron.

Body and analytical text balance the display weight with neutral, legible sans-serif geometry. Uppercase styling with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) is reserved for navigational headers, badges, telemetry tags, and tactical labels.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column fluid grid orchestrates structural hierarchy on desktop, consolidating down to a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column compact flow on mobile. Layout margins contract on smaller displays to protect precious horizontal territory for condensed display typography.

Spacing rhythms oscillate deliberately between tight, regimented internal component spacing (tactical clusters) and expansive macro gaps that give heroic athletic photography breathing room. Structural elements align flush against strict horizontal and vertical planes, reinforcing military precision.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons traditional drop shadows in favor of ambient volcanic rim illumination and deep tonal layering:

- **Base Layer (`#080808`)**: Deepest black canvas plane.
- **Surface Elevation (`#151515`)**: Rigid geometric containers layered directly above the base.
- **Atmospheric Glow**: Accent lighting created through multi-stop radiant radial gradients (`rgba(240, 68, 53, 0.25)` to `rgba(255, 148, 26, 0.05)` to `transparent`) layered behind key character cutouts or structural panels.
- **Edge Definition**: Surfaces and cards use razor-sharp 1px structural outlines (`#262626` or semi-transparent red highlights `rgba(240, 68, 53, 0.4)`) instead of soft elevation blurs.

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly angular, unyielding, and tactical. Standard border radiuses are set to `0px`.

Interactive controls and focal containers utilize a signature **angular chamfered cut** (`polygon(12px 0%, 100% 0%, calc(100% - 12px) 100%, 0% 100%)` for parallelogram buttons, or 45-degree corner clippings via `clip-path: polygon(...)`). This sharp profile reinforces the athletic, industrial motif across all interactive elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Scarlet (`#F04435`) background transitioning to Ember (`#FF941A`) on hover. Angular clipped corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)` or slanted parallelogram cut). Pure white, uppercase, bold tracking (`label-lg`). Includes subtle outward fiery glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(240, 68, 53, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Dark charcoal (`#151515`) fill with 1px border (`#FFFFFF` or `#F04435`). Identical angular silhouette. Inverts fill and text on interaction.

### Quote & Testimonial Panels
- Grounded on `#151515` background with 1px border (`#262626`) featuring an asymmetrical chamfered top-left or bottom-right corner.
- Massive quotation marks styled in high-contrast white or scarlet. Text styled with high-contrast dual coloring (e.g., body white with key phrases accented in Ember/Scarlet).

### Badges & Chips
- Flat, rectangular or single-chamfer indicators. Dark translucent fill with saturated red or orange borders. Typography set to uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`).

### Input Fields
- Monolithic `#101010` inputs with crisp 1px borders (`#262626`). Focus state transitions border to `#F04435` with an internal 1px fiery hairline glow. Text is crisp white with muted `#666666` placeholder text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Sharp rectangular checkboxes and diamond-shaped radio markers. Unchecked states hold a 1px border in `#333333`; checked states ignite in solid `#F04435` with pure white indicator glyphs.
